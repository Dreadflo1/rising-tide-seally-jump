Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before your first jump.

Is Rising Tide: Seally Jump free to play?
Yes — it's completely free and plays right in your browser on desktop or mobile. No download and no sign-up required; ads keep it free.
How do I play?
Steer left and right — Seally bounces up automatically. Grab pearls, dodge the ocean hazards, and stay ahead of the rising tide. On desktop use the Left/Right arrow keys; on mobile tap the left or right side of the screen.
Do I need to install anything?
No. It runs instantly in any modern browser. You can also add it to your home screen as an app (PWA) to play offline and launch it like a native game.
How does playing help the ocean?
20% of the game's revenue is donated to The Ocean Cleanup, which removes plastic from oceans and rivers. Just playing for free already helps.
Can I keep my pearls, skins and high score?
Yes — your progress saves automatically. Create a free account with Google or email to sync it across devices and claim your spot on the global leaderboard.
Where else can I play it?
You can also find Seally Jump on game portals such as itch.io and GameDistribution. seally.best is its official home.
